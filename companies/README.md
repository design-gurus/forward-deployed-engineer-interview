# Loops by Company

None of these companies publishes a fixed forward deployed engineer loop. Every fact on these pages is commonly reported by candidates and preparation guides, and loops change often. Confirm the stages, and the rules on AI tools, with your recruiter. This repository is not affiliated with any company it names.

| Company | Role title | Reported stages | Stage that decides most results | Reported length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Palantir](palantir.md) | Forward deployed software engineer | Recruiter call, technical screen, three onsite rounds from five types, hiring manager | Decomposition | About a month |
| [OpenAI](openai.md) | Forward deployed engineer | Recruiter screen, take-home with a coding screen, four to six onsite interviews | Take-home case study | Three to six weeks |
| [Anthropic](anthropic.md) | Forward deployed engineer | Recruiter screen, use-case screen, coding, hiring manager, solution design, final panel | Final values panel | Four to six weeks |
| [Google](google.md) | Forward deployed engineer, levels I to V and Staff | Recruiter screen, coding, "vibe coding", agentic design, Googleyness, hiring committee, team match | Hiring committee review of all rounds | Six to eight weeks |
| [Cognition](cognition.md) | Forward deployed engineer or deployed engineer | Recruiter screen, product take-home, presentation, leadership conversations, executive pitch, customer call | The customer simulations | Not reliably reported |

## Which rounds each loop uses

| Round | Palantir | OpenAI | Anthropic | Google | Cognition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Decomposition](../rounds/decomposition.md) or customer case | Yes | Inside system design | Inside solution design | Role-play reported once | Customer call |
| [Learning](../rounds/learning-round.md) | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| [Re-engineering](../rounds/re-engineering-round.md) | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| [Practical coding](../rounds/practical-coding.md) | Yes | Yes, staged | Yes, staged | Algorithms and "vibe coding" | Little |
| [Applied AI design](../rounds/applied-ai-system-design.md) | Data pipelines | Yes | Yes | Yes, agentic | No |
| [Take-home](../rounds/take-home-project.md) | No | Yes | No | No | Yes |
| [Project deep dive](../rounds/project-deep-dive.md) | No | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| [Behavioral](../rounds/behavioral-for-customer-work.md) | In every round | Yes | Values panel | Googleyness | Leadership conversations |

"No" means the round is not commonly reported for that loop, not that it never occurs.
