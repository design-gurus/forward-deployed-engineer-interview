# Four-Week Plan for the FDE Interview

This plan uses about 8 hours a week for four weeks, which is 32 hours. It gives the most time to decomposition, because that round appears in nearly every loop in some form and rejects the most strong coders. The plan assumes a candidate who already writes code comfortably.

## The split

| Skill | Hours | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Decomposition, discovery, and scoping | 12 | 38 percent |
| Learning and re-engineering | 8 | 25 percent |
| Practical coding, SQL, and APIs | 8 | 25 percent |
| Stories, motivation, and a full mock | 4 | 12 percent |

## Week by week

| Week | Focus | Work | Finished when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decomposition method | Read [decomposition](../rounds/decomposition.md) and [discovery and scoping](../rounds/discovery-and-scoping.md). Work three [practice problems](decomposition-practice-problems.md), 60 minutes each, aloud. | You ask about the user and the data before proposing anything, without a reminder |
| 2 | Unfamiliar code | Four 45-minute sessions with a library you have never used. Two debugging sessions on code written by someone else. See [learning](../rounds/learning-round.md) and [re-engineering](../rounds/re-engineering-round.md). | You reproduce a fault before changing any code |
| 3 | Practical coding | Three data-cleaning tasks, two API wrappers, ten SQL queries, and three staged problems. See [practical coding](../rounds/practical-coding.md). | A staged problem reaches stage 3 without a rewrite |
| 4 | Full rehearsal | Two timed decomposition rounds with a partner (4 hours). Six stories and two motivation answers (4 hours). See [behavioral questions](../rounds/behavioral-for-customer-work.md). | You can answer "Why FDE?" and "Why this company?" as two different answers |

## Adjust for the company

Move hours, and keep the total at 32.

| Company | Change to the plan |
|---|---|
| [Palantir](../companies/palantir.md) | Keep the plan as written, and practice every coding task without AI tools |
| [OpenAI](../companies/openai.md) | Move 4 hours from week 2 to [applied AI system design](../rounds/applied-ai-system-design.md), and build one small project with a test set before the take-home arrives |
| [Anthropic](../companies/anthropic.md) | Move 4 hours from week 2 to applied AI design, and add 2 hours in week 4 for the values panel |
| [Google](../companies/google.md) | Replace week 2 with algorithm practice in a plain document, and spend 3 hours of week 3 on tasks whose specification changes |
| [Cognition](../companies/cognition.md) | Replace week 2 with product use and one executive pitch, and replace week 3 with a 45-minute project talk |

## If you have one week

With 10 hours, do the following in order:

1. Three hours: two decomposition problems aloud, timed.
2. Two hours: one staged coding problem and five SQL queries.
3. Two hours: the page for your company, and its design or take-home format.
4. Two hours: four stories with numbers.
5. One hour: the two motivation answers, spoken to another person.

## A daily habit

Each day, take one request from your own work and write three lines: the user, the decision, and the number that would show success. It takes five minutes, and after four weeks the first step of every customer round is automatic.

## How to tell that you are ready

- You can state a one-sentence scope for a new problem within 17 minutes.
- Your decomposition answers name what is left out, with a reason.
- You can explain one past project three levels deep on every decision.
- Every story ends in a number.
